Pourover Wizard

A guided V60 pour-over timer. Pick a recipe, set your coffee dose and follow step-by-step pour cues. Connect a supported Bluetooth coffee scale and the guide shows your pour against the target in real time.

Recipes: Better 1 Cup V60, Ultimate V60, 4:6 Method. Works offline, with no account and no analytics. Live scale assist needs Web Bluetooth (Chrome or Edge on desktop or Android, Bluefy on iOS).
